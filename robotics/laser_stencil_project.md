# ✂️ Laser: Stencil or Engrave
### Engrave it, cut it out, paint through it, or stamp it on a T-shirt

> **Grades:** 7 to 12 · **Time:** 1 to 2 periods plus laser time · **Difficulty:** Beginner · **Grade:** Optional (skipped = Exempt)
> The full student lesson, with all 35 example designs, is on the site: **thinkinbits.site/cs/fabrication/laser-stencil/**

## What You'll Learn
- Why the laser cuts SVGs but can only engrave PNGs and JPGs
- Four things it makes: engraving, cut-out, stencil, eraser stamp
- Why stencils need bridges (islands fall out without them)
- Laser safety

## What You Need
- A design (example, found picture, AI picture, or drawing). Logos and characters are allowed; keep it school appropriate.
- pngtosvg.com or Inkscape to make an SVG, or Mr. Krigger converts it
- Stencil/stamp: plain cotton T-shirt (or tote/notebook) and acrylic paint. Stamp: a pink block eraser.

## Safety
Adult in the room, lid closed, someone watching the whole time. No vinyl or PVC ever. Pink rubber erasers only. Flame that keeps going or smoke that will not clear: tell Mr. Krigger.

## Steps
1. Turn the picture into an SVG if it will be cut (pngtosvg.com or Inkscape Trace Bitmap).
2. Size to 100 mm or less; stencils: bridge every island (2 mm minimum).
3. Upload to Schoology or email akrigger@ghcds.org; say engrave, cut-out, stencil or stamp.
4. Use it; photo it.

## Base and Stretch
- **Base (everyone):** one finished engraving, cut-out, stencil or stamp within 100 x 100 mm.
- **Stretch (HS required, MS +3):** own drawing or a real change to a picture/example; stencils bridged by the student. Say what changed.

## Deliverables
Design file · photo of the finished piece (stencil/stamp: something painted with it) · one sentence on the choice (Stretch: what changed) · crew list. Crews of 1 to 3, each student turns in.

## Grading Rubric (25)
| Category | Points | Full marks |
|---|---|---|
| Working piece | 10 | Engraved, cut, stenciled or stamped; fits 100 x 100 mm; photo shows it |
| Design choices | 5 | Kind of piece fits the design. HS: own design or real change; stencil bridges work |
| Understanding | 5 | Sentence explains the choice; Stretch says what changed |
| Turn-in | 5 | File, photo, sentence, crew, named with section and initials |
| MS bonus | +3 | Stretch done |
