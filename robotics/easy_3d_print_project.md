# 🧊 Easy 3D Print
### See what a 3D printer can really do, then pick one thing to print and keep

> **Grades:** 7 to 12 · **Time:** 1 period plus print time · **Difficulty:** Beginner · **Grade:** Optional (skipped = Exempt)
> The full student lesson, with every example file, is on the site: **thinkinbits.site/cs/fabrication/easy-print/**

## What You'll Learn
- Where models come from: libraries, own design, code, AI
- Matching a model to a printer or resin (Clear V4, Tough 2000, MakerBot PLA)
- Checking a model will print: size, thin parts, time

## What You Need
A model from MakerWorld, Printables, Thingiverse, Tripo3D, Tinkercad, OpenSCAD or Blender, or a picture for Mr. Krigger to run through Tripo3D.

## Safety
Gloves (nitrile/neoprene) and glasses with liquid resin; only safety-checked students wash, cure or remove supports, with Mr. Krigger present; MakerBot nozzle about 200 C; battery tealights only in the Clear shade.

## Checks
Fits 50 mm (Formlabs) or 80 mm (MakerBot) · prints in 2 hours or less · nothing thinner than about 1 mm · no weapons or look-alikes · named `HS-JK-easyprint.stl`.

## Base and Stretch
- **Base (everyone):** one print from any source that passes the checks.
- **Stretch (HS required, MS +3):** own design or a real change to a downloaded/AI model. Say what changed.

## Deliverables
STL, link, or the picture given to Mr. Krigger · photo of the print · one sentence on printer/resin choice (Stretch: what changed) · crew list. Crews of 1 to 3, each student turns in.

## Grading Rubric (25)
| Category | Points | Full marks |
|---|---|---|
| Working print | 10 | Printed, cleaned, passes size and time checks; photo shows it |
| Design choices | 5 | Printer or resin fits the model. HS: own design or real change |
| Understanding | 5 | Sentence explains the printer/resin; Stretch says what changed |
| Turn-in | 5 | File/link/picture, photo, sentence, crew, named with section and initials |
| MS bonus | +3 | Stretch done |

## Example files (on the site)
Clear V4: glass gyroid, tealight shade, lattice ball, sea-glass crystal, Moon (hollow, LED hole), Saturn, DNA helix, trefoil knot. Tough 2000: screw and nut, flex clip, gear set, rocket, chess king/rook/pawn. MakerBot: twisted vase (+ .scad), spiral shell (Blender script), sea turtle.

## Vocabulary
STL, slicer, resin, supports, watertight, parametric.
