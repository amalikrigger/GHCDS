| Box | Example |
|---|---|
| **The problem** | The charging cable for the lab iPad cart falls behind the desk every day. |
| **Who it is for** | Mr. Krigger. He said "I fish it out with a ruler twice a week." |
| **My design** | A C-shaped clip that slides onto the desk edge, with a 6 mm slot on top that holds the cable. |
| **Measurements** | Desk edge is 18 mm, so the clip opening is 18.5 mm. The cable is 5.5 mm, so the slot is 6 mm. |

| **What went wrong, what I changed** | Version 1's slot was too tight. I widened it by 0.5 mm. |
