## Answer Key (Teacher Copy)

1. **B.** Short demonstration at the machine, unlocks it for their own projects during class and academic time. It is retakeable.

2. **B.** The plate is heated and stays hot, and the nozzle sits right above where their hand goes. Answer D is worth discussing, since cutting power mid-cool is not dangerous but does not speed anything up.

3. **B.** **This is the gating question.** A student who does not know the vinyl rule does not get laser access yet. The gas is chlorine based, it harms people and corrodes the machine from the inside, and the material looks completely harmless, which is exactly why the rule exists. Verified against Glowforge's own materials guidance.

4. **B.** Rule six. If a student picks A or C, that is worth a conversation rather than just a mark. Almost every serious workshop accident is a small problem somebody chose not to mention.

5. **C.** A swollen lithium battery is a damaged one, and damaged lithium batteries vent and burn. Never charge it, never use it, never put it back on the shelf where someone else will pick it up.

6. **B.** Sensitization is the real reason, and it is permanent. "To keep your hands clean" is the answer that shows they have not understood why it is not optional.

7. Any five of: tie back hair and sleeves; eye protection when cutting or soldering; never leave a running machine unattended, never reach into a moving printer, never override a guard; laser only with an adult in the room; charge batteries at the station, in view, not overnight; report damage, burn smell or injury immediately and you will not be in trouble; breaking a rule on purpose costs tool access and earns a 0.
   *In their own words. Copied wording from the page earns half.*

8. Any tool, correctly matched to its check. Printer safety check, handling and power check, driving and battery care check, or the Glowforge independent learning certification.
