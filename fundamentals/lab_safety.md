# 🦺 Lab Safety and Earning Tool Access

### Nothing in this room is locked away from you. It is waiting for you to earn it.

> **Grades:** 7th to 12th &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Time:** 2 class periods &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Difficulty:** Beginner
>
> Most school labs work by keeping students away from the good equipment. This one does the opposite. Every machine here is something you can earn the right to use on your own projects, during class and during academic time. The trade is simple. You learn how each machine hurts you, you prove you can shut it down safely, and then it is yours.

Full student version, including the eight situations, is on the class website:
**thinkinbits.site/cs/fundamentals/lab-safety/**

---

## ⚠️ Safety Rules, Quoted From the Syllabus

These are non-negotiable and they cover every tool in this room.

1. **Tie back long hair and push up loose sleeves** before using anything that spins, cuts, or heats.
2. **Eye protection** when cutting or soldering.
3. **Never leave a running machine unattended**, never reach into a printer that is moving, and never override a lid switch or guard.
4. **The laser cutter is only used with an adult in the room.**
5. **Charge batteries at the charging station**, in view, not overnight.
6. **Report damage, a burn smell, or an injury right away.** You will not be in trouble for reporting something. You will be for hiding it.
7. **Breaking a safety rule on purpose** costs you tool access and earns a 0 for the day. Getting access back means retaking the check.

---

## 🎯 What You'll Learn

- The seven rules that apply to every tool in this room
- How a **safety check** works and what it unlocks
- The specific way each machine can hurt you or itself
- The one material that must never go in the laser cutter
- What to do when something goes wrong, and why reporting protects you

---

## 🧰 What You Need

- Yourself, in the room, paying attention. This lesson happens at the machines.
- Long hair tied back and loose sleeves pushed up before you touch anything.

# Day 1: The Rules and the Deal

**Goal:** you know the seven rules without looking, you know how each machine can hurt you, and you know what to do to unlock each one.

### What each check unlocks

| Tool | What you pass first | Then you may |
|---|---|---|
| 3D printers (MakerBot, Formlabs) | Printer safety check | Slice and queue your own prints in class and during academic time |
| micro:bit and Raspberry Pi kits | Handling and power check | Sign out a kit for a project |
| RoboMaster | Driving and battery care check | Build, drive and program it for your own projects |
| Glowforge laser cutter | Independent learning certification | Cut your own designs, with an adult in the room |

### How each machine hurts you

| Machine | The actual hazard | What you do about it |
|---|---|---|
| **MakerBot** | Nozzle around 200°C, heated build plate, gantry moves without warning | Hands out while it moves. Let the plate cool. Scraper away from your other hand. |
| **Formlabs** | Liquid resin irritates skin and can sensitize you permanently. IPA vapor is flammable. | Nitrile gloves for anything wet, every time. Bottles closed. Soap and water immediately on skin contact. |
| **Glowforge** | A laser that burns wood, a real fire risk, and some materials release poison gas | Adult in the room. Lid shut. You stay and watch. Approved materials only. |
| **RoboMaster** | Fires gel beads. Battery can vent or burn if damaged. | Eye protection during blaster events, never aim at a face, charge at the station, report a swollen battery. |
| **micro:bit, Pi, CrowPi** | Low risk. Mostly static and wrong voltage damaging the board. | Hold by the edges. Power down before rewiring. Check voltage first. |

### The material that must never go in the laser

**Never cut or engrave vinyl or PVC.** Glowforge states it plainly: cutting vinyl "can release hazardous gases that can hurt you and damage your Glowforge." The gas is chlorine based. It corrodes the machine from the inside and it is dangerous to breathe.

Also never: **galvanized metal**, which releases hazardous gases when cut, and **shiny soft metals** (copper, chrome, gold, silver, brass), which reflect the beam rather than absorbing it.

> **The rule that actually works:** if the material did not come from Mr. Krigger, it does not go in the laser until he has looked at it. Sticker sheets and vinyl wrap from home are exactly what this rule exists to stop.

*Checked against Glowforge's own materials guidance on 2026-09-10.*

> **✅ Checkpoint:** they can say what happens if vinyl goes in the Glowforge, and why nobody would notice until it was too late.

# Day 2: Earn Your First Check

**Goal:** they have passed at least one safety check and unlocked a tool.

### What a check looks like

1. Mr. Krigger shows the tool and the parts that matter
2. **The student** runs it, watched
3. **The student** shuts it down safely and puts it back
4. A few questions, including at least one about what to do when something goes wrong

It is retakeable. Nobody is permanently locked out for not passing first time. Only breaking a rule on purpose costs access for real.

> **✅ Checkpoint:** at least one check passed.

---

## 🎚️ Base and Stretch

**Base, everyone does this.**
The shutdown procedure for the tool they were checked out on, written from memory, in order, plus the one thing a person is most likely to get wrong.

**Stretch, required for high school, bonus for middle school.**
Add the emergency version: what to do if it starts smoking, jams, or a part comes loose mid-run, including where the power is and who to tell.

## 🧠 Vocabulary to Know

| Term | What It Means |
|---|---|
| **Safety check** | A short demonstration at the machine that unlocks it. Graded, and retakeable. |
| **Guard** | A cover or switch that stops a machine when opened. Overriding one costs access. |
| **Build plate** | The surface a print is built on. Heated on the MakerBot, so it stays hot after. |
| **Resin** | The liquid a Formlabs builds from. Irritates skin, and repeated contact sensitizes you permanently. |
| **IPA** | Isopropyl alcohol, used to wash resin prints. Vapor is flammable, so bottles stay closed. |
| **PVC and vinyl** | The plastic that must never go in the laser cutter. Releases chlorine based gas. |
| **Sensitize** | To develop a permanent allergic reaction after repeated exposure. Why gloves are not optional. |
| **Venting** | What a damaged lithium battery does before it burns. Swollen means damaged. |
